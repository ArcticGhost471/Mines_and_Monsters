Radiant Rebirth. When the dragon dies, its body erupts in radiant beams of purple light. Each creature within 60 feet of it must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or be blinded for 1 minute. A blinded creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

 The dragon's body then implodes, leaving behind an inert, jet-black dragon egg that weighs 30 pounds. The egg is a small object with AC 20; 20 hit points; and immunity to all damage. A creature that attempts to touch the dragon egg must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity check or the egg teleports to a random unoccupied space within 30 feet.

 The dragon ressurects if a ritual is completed within its lair. Four end crystals must be placed placed at the edges of the color pool at the center of the dragon's lair. If the dragon egg is then dropped into the color pool while the end crystals are in place, all end crystals in the dragon's lair emit a beam of light, converging above the color pool, and the dragon gains a new body immediately, reviving with all its hit points. The new body appears in an unoccupied space within the dragon's lair. Both the dragon egg and the end crystals used in the ritual disappear once the dragon revives.


Legendary Resistance (4/day). If the dragon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.


Actions

Multiattack. It then makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.


Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (2d10 + 8) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.


Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 8) slashing damage.


Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage.


Harmful Breath (Recharge 5-6). The dragon exhales putrid gas in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must make a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 52 (15d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The dragon then chooses a point it can see where the cone makes contact with the ground. That point becomes the center of a 20-foot-high, 20-foot-radius cylinder of lingering gas that disappears at the start of the dragon's next turn. Any creature that starts its turn in the cylinder takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage.


Bonus Actions

Immaterial Passing. The dragon enters an incorporeal-like for as long as it maintains concentration on the effect (as if concentrating on a spell). While incorporeal, the dragon can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.


Legendary Actions

The dragon can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The dragon regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.


Attack. The dragon makes one claw or tail attack.

Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon beats its wings. Each creature within 15 feet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw or take 15 (2d6 + 8) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. The dragon can then fly up to half its flying speed.

Voidfire Ball (Costs 3 Actions). The dragon unleashes an ball of void-tainted necrotic energy that detonates in a 20-foot-radius sphere at a point it can see within 120 feet of it. Each creature within that sphere must make a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.


An Ender Dragon's Lair

The mighty Ender dragon originates from the Astral Plane, where they can be found among an archipelago of petrified rock within the astral sea. They fly above a central island surrounded by expansive rings of islets of which they never seem to fly beyond. No one can tell whether they are a guardian or prisoner of their domain, but either way, the Ender Dragon challenges anyone who enters all the same.

 On the center island stand ten massive obsidian pillars arranged in a wide circle formation approximately 150 feet from the center. These pillars are of varying height, each between 60 to 150 feet tall and 20 to 40 feet wide in diameter at their base. Atop each pillar hovers a Large crystal object engraved with strange, glowing runes pulsing with healing magic. At the center of the island lies a color pool leading to the material plane.


Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the dragon takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; the dragon can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

  • Crystal Healing. A crystal atop an obsidian pillar within 120 feet of the dragon begins channeling healing energy, creating a visible beam linking it to the dragon. At initiative count 20 on the next round, the dragon regains 60 hit points. If the crystal is destroyed before the effect resolves, the dragon instead takes 14 (4d6) force damage, and the crystal explodes in a 20-foot-radius sphere. Any creature in that area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Each crystal is a Large object hovering above the obsidian pillar and has AC 11; 30 hit points; vulnerability to bludgeoning and thunder damage; resistance to piercing and slashing damage; and immunity to poison and psychic damage.
  • Displacing Gateway. A gateway lights up somewhere in the dragon's lair. One creature the dragon can see within the lair must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or be teleported, appearing within 5 feet of the lit up gateway.
  • Obsidian Skin. The dragon casts stoneskin (no components required) on itself. While maintaining concentration on this effect, the dragon can't take other lair actions.

Regional Effect

The Ender Dragon's presence warps its surroundings in strange ways, creating one or more of the following effects:

  • Aberrant Barrens. The land within 1 miles of the lair becomes barren and desolate, covering the land in pale yellow. Purple, coral-like chorus plants grow in abundance in the area. A creature that eats its fruit must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be teleported up to 30 feet to a random unoccupied space.
  • End Gateways. Interdimensional gateways appear within 6 mile of the lair as floating, 15-foot-tall octahedrons reflecting an astral sky. Each gateway is linked to another. A creature or object that passes through one gateway emerges from a linked gateway within 5 feet of it. A gateway can be used by any creature and remains active indefinitely.
  • Necrotic Mist. A concealing fog obscures the area within 500 feet of the dragon's lair. Piercing violet eyes occasionally pierce through the darkness of the fog.

If the Ender Dragon dies, chorus plants remain as it has grown. The end gateways close over the course of 1d10 days. The fog dissipates immediately.

Ender Dragon's Lair

The Ender Dragon resides at the center island of an archipelago of petrified rock in the Astral Plane. On the island stand ten obsidian pillars arranged in a circler formation 150 feet from the center of the lair. Each pillar is 40 feet tall and 20 feet wide in diameter at their base. Atop each pillar hovers a Large crystal object that provides healing for the Ender Dragon. A color pool leading to the material plane lies at the center of the dragon's lair.


Regional Effects

The region containing the Ender Dragon's lair is warped by its presence, creating the following effects:


End Gateways. Interdimensional gateways appear within 1 mile of the lair as floating, 15-foot-tall octahedrons reflecting an astral sky. Each gateway is linked to another. A creature or object that passes through one gateway emerges from a linked gateway within 5 feet of it. A gateway can be used by any creature and remains active indefinitely.

If the dragon dies or moves its lair elsewhere, these effects end immediately.
