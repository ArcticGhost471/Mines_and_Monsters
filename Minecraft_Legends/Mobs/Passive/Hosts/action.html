Legendary Actions

Action can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Action regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.


Ethereal Form. Action uses Etherealness.

Slam. Action makes one slam attack.

Aura of Courage. Action envigorates all of her allies within 60 feet of her until the end of her next turn. Envigorated creatures can't be frightened, and they gain advantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of Action's next turn.


The Host's Lair

The Hosts take residence in the Well of Fate, a celestial temple located in the Ethereal Plane. It exists in the Border Ethereal where the hosts can peer into the Material Plane without interfering in the affairs of mortals. The lair and its inhabitants can be perceived by creatures on the Material Plane; they can interact with the structure, but creatures within remain intangible while they are in the Border Ethereal.


Regional Effect

The region containing the Host's lair is warped by its magic, creating one or more of the following effects:

  • Ethereal Presence. Creatures not native to the Ethereal Plane have a translucent appearance while in the lair.
  • Portals of Fate. Once per day, the Host can open a portal linking to another plane of existence. They don't need to know the name of a specific creature, otherwise the effect is identical to the gate spell.
  • Regeneration Stones. Mysterious stone formations magically come into existance within 6 miles of the lair. These stones emit restorative energy within 10 feet of themselves, revitalizing creatures nearby as a common potion of healing would.

If the Host dies, these effects fade over the course of 1d10 days.
